### **7-Day Japan Itinerary: Tokyo & Kyoto Focus**
*Optimized for cost savings by minimizing long-distance train fares.*

#### **Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive at Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND). Take the **Narita Express** or **Keisei Skyliner** to central Tokyo.
*   **Evening:** Check into hotel in **Shinjuku** (best for budget, food, and transport).
*   **Sightseeing:** 
    - Dotonbori-style street food in Shinjuku.
    - Visit **Meiji Shrine** (free) if time permits.
*   **Stay:** Shinjuku (Budget: ₹4,000)

#### **Day 2: Tokyo – Modern & Historic**
*   **Morning:** **Senso-ji Temple** in Asakusa (free). Take the river taxi or metro.
*   **Afternoon:** **Akihabara** (Electric Town) – free walking, cheap snacks.
*   **Evening:** **Shibuya Scramble Crossing** + **Harajuku** (free).
*   **Food:** Conveyor belt sushi (₹1,500–₹2,000).
*   **Stay:** Shinjuku

#### **Day 3: Tokyo – Nature & Culture**
*   **Morning:** **Ueno Park** & **National Museum** (₹800 entry).
*   **Afternoon:** **Imperial Palace East Gardens** (free).
*   **Evening:** **Tokyo Skytree** (optional, ₹1,200) or stay in **Roppongi** for nightlife.
*   **Stay:** Shinjuku

#### **Day 4: Tokyo to Kyoto (Shinkansen)**
*   **Morning:** Take **Shinkansen** (Bullet Train) from Tokyo to Kyoto.
    - *Cost:* ~₹12,000–₹14,000 for a non-reserved seat. *Tip: Book via SmartEX or Japan Rail Pass alternative.*
*   **Afternoon:** Check into hotel in **Kyoto Station** area.
*   **Evening:** **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (free, iconic torii gates). Go at sunset to avoid crowds.
*   **Stay:** Kyoto (Budget: ₹4,000)

#### **Day 5: Kyoto – Temples & Traditions**
*   **Morning:** **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion) – ₹600 entry.
*   **Afternoon:** **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** (free) + **Tenryu-ji Temple** (₹800).
*   **Evening:** **Gion District** walk (free chance to see geiko).
*   **Food:** Ramen (₹800–₹1,000).
*   **Stay:** Kyoto

#### **Day 6: Kyoto – Zen Gardens**
*   **Morning:** **Ryoan-ji Zen Rock Garden** (₹600).
*   **Afternoon:** **Nijo Castle** (₹900).
*   **Evening:** **Pontocho Alley** for dinner (budget-friendly izakaya).
*   **Stay:** Kyoto

#### **Day 7: Kyoto to Tokyo & Departure**
*   **Morning:** Last-minute shopping in **Kyoto Station** (souvenirs, snacks).
*   **Afternoon:** Take **Shinkansen** back to Tokyo (Haneda/Narita).
    - *Cost:* ~₹12,000–₹14,000.
*   **Evening:** International flight back to India.
